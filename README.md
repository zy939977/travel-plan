# Japan Travel Plan

Welcome to the Japan Travel Plan repository! This project helps you organize and plan your journey through Japan.

## 📍 Overview

This repository contains resources, itineraries, and planning tools for traveling to Japan. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a returning explorer, this project provides guidance for making the most of your trip.

## ✨ Features

- **Itinerary Planning**: Organize your travel route and activities
- **Location Guides**: Information about popular destinations across Japan
- **Travel Tips**: Practical advice for navigating Japan
- **Transportation**: Details about trains, buses, and local transit

## 🗾 Getting Started

1. Clone this repository or explore the existing content
2. Review the documentation and guides
3. Plan your itinerary based on your interests and timeframe
4. Share your experiences and improvements

## 🌸 Popular Destinations

- **Tokyo** - Capital city with modern attractions and cultural sites
- **Kyoto** - Historic temples, gardens, and traditional culture
- **Osaka** - Vibrant food scene and entertainment hub
- **Mount Fuji** - Iconic mountain with hiking opportunities
- **Hiroshima** - Historic significance and beautiful riverside location

## 📚 Resources

- Official Japan Tourism Board: https://www.jnto.go.jp/
- Japan Rail Pass Information: https://www.jrpass.com/
- Local Event Calendars: Check specific prefectures for seasonal events

## 💡 Tips

- Best times to visit: Spring (cherry blossoms) and Fall (autumn foliage)
- Learn basic Japanese phrases for a better experience
- Plan accommodation and transportation in advance during peak seasons
- Consider obtaining a Japan Rail Pass for extensive train travel
